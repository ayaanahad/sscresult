Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 August 2026

Who we are

Folafol (ফলাফল) is an independent Android app that helps students and guardians in Bangladesh check public examination results — JSC/JDC, SSC/Dakhil, HSC/Alim and equivalent — from all education boards. Folafol is published by Tech Internal. Folafol is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any education board or government body of Bangladesh.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask you to create an account. We do not collect your name, email address (unless you choose to email us), phone number, contacts, photos, or camera/microphone data, and we do not request precise (GPS) location.

Stored on your device only

  • Roll numbers and exam details you enter or save
  • Language and theme preferences
  • Saved rolls and notification subscriptions

This information stays on your device and is not uploaded to us with your identity.

Stored on our servers

To make repeat lookups fast, a published result is cached on our infrastructure (Cloudflare Workers KV) for up to 12 months. The cache is keyed by exam, year, board, roll and registration number. We do not log who performed a search.

Third-party services

  • Google Firebase — anonymous usage analytics, crash reporting and push notifications
  • Google AdMob and mediation partners, including Meta Audience Network — advertising
  • Meta — app-event measurement (when enabled)
  • Cloudflare — hosting and delivery infrastructure
  • Education boards — the source of the results you request

Advertising and advertising ID

Ads are served by Google AdMob and its mediation partners (including Meta Audience Network) using a resettable advertising identifier and approximate, IP-based location. Your roll number, registration number and results are never shared with advertisers. Users below the regional age of consent are served non-personalised ads. You can reset your advertising ID at any time in your device settings.

Analytics

Firebase Analytics records anonymous usage events (such as which screens are opened) to help us improve the app. We never log roll or registration numbers in analytics.

Children

Folafol is intended for users aged 13 and above. Many of our users are secondary and higher-secondary students; we serve non-personalised ads to users below the applicable age of consent and do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and data deletion

  • Delete saved rolls and preferences at any time inside the app
  • Reset or limit your advertising ID in device settings
  • Email us to request removal of a cached result from our servers

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
